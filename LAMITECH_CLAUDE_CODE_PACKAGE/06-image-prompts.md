## Zasada wspólna

Obrazy mają tworzyć jedną spójną serię: nowoczesne, realne wnętrza w Polsce, zabudowy dopasowane do przestrzeni, naturalne materiały, spokojna architektoniczna fotografia. Bez marek, tekstów i kopiowania istniejących realizacji.

## 1. Hero

- Plik: `hero-przestrzen-domknieta-meblem-16x9.webp`
- Miejsce: strona główna
- Format: 16:9
- Alt: Zabudowa meblowa dopasowana do jasnego wnętrza
- Fraza stockowa: `custom built-in cabinetry architectural interior natural light Poland`

### Prompt

Create one photorealistic premium architectural interior image for the hero section of a custom furniture workshop website in Poland. Show a contemporary but attainable apartment where a full-height custom built-in seamlessly completes the architecture of the room: integrated storage, one open niche, a subtle desk or seating element and carefully aligned vertical panels. The furniture must clearly be made for the exact wall dimensions, not a freestanding showroom product. Wide eye-level composition, 35 mm full-frame lens, camera centered slightly left, main cabinetry on the right two-thirds, generous quiet negative space on the left for a large headline. Soft natural morning daylight, warm birch veneer, muted sage-grey lacquer, mineral off-white walls and small graphite details. Refined but not luxurious, calm and highly functional. Straight verticals, realistic apartment proportions, high material fidelity. No people, no text, no logo, no watermark, no brand names, no excessive marble, no gold, no impossible joinery, no floating cabinets, no glossy CGI. Single image, 16:9, high resolution.
